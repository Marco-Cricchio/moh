## Allowed (API/CLI usage)

- Use Zen and Go through their documented API-key interfaces within the applicable account and plan conditions.
- Retain rights in your content; third-party model terms still govern the models you use.

## Forbidden (API/CLI usage)

- Do not bypass account, subscription, balance, or API-key controls, or resell access contrary to the hosted-service terms.
- Do not equate the open-source client license with hosted Zen/Go permissions, or assume every model has the same privacy policy.

## Data handling

- **Retention:** Basic proxied prompts are passed upstream rather than stored by OpenCode. Hosted-agent sessions are an exception, normally deleted after 30 days of inactivity; account data and other hosted features have separate retention. Upstream model retention still applies.
- **Training on your inputs:** Terms permit unpaid-account content to improve the service, except content obtained/derived from Connected Services. Connected-Service data is not used to train models by OpenCode. Upstream exceptions, including some free models, mean no universal no-training or ZDR guarantee.
